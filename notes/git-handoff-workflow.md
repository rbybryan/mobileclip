# Private operational notes

Live machine handoffs and session transcripts are excluded from the release tree and
ignored under `notes/live/`. Do not commit credentials, private access instructions,
shared-machine details, or chat transcripts to a public branch.

Historical runbooks may refer to `notes/live/from-server.md` or `from-claude.md`.
Those files are not distributed and their old branch-sync commands are not part of the
public reproduction workflow. For supported entry points, use the [README](../README.md).

Record scientific results, configurations, and reproducible commands in the relevant
experiment report. Keep operational chatter in a private channel or local ignored file.
